FAQ

Questions freelancers ask before switching

Still deciding?Our support team replies within a few hours. Ask a question
Do my clients need a Ledgerly account to pay?

No. They get an email with a link to the invoice and can pay by card or bank transfer without signing up.

When does the money reach my bank account?

Card payments arrive in 2 business days. Bank transfers take 3 to 5 business days, depending on the client’s bank.

Can I import clients and past invoices?

Yes. Upload a CSV of clients and invoices, or connect your previous invoicing tool and we copy them across, including payment status.

What happens if I go over 3 clients on Starter?

Nothing breaks. You can archive a finished client to free up a slot, or upgrade to Pro. Existing invoices keep working either way.

Does Ledgerly handle sales tax and VAT?

You can add tax rates per client or per line item, and the totals and reports show tax collected separately.
