▸ 00  Self, in the mirrorCihapit · 2026

I photograph people the way Bandung looks at six in the morning: quietly, and a little out of the fog.

I'm Raka. I grew up in Cihapit, learned to print in a borrowed darkroom on Jalan Braga, and have been photographing weddings and portraits around West Java since 2015.

I work alone or with one second shooter, with two small cameras and no lighting crew. I won't ask you to repeat a moment. I'd rather be standing in the right place when it happens the first time.

I take on about twenty-four weddings a year so that every gallery is edited by me, frame by frame.

Nadia & BagasFrame 01 · Pangalengan, June 2026
  1. 01

    How far ahead should we book?

    Most couples write eight to twelve months before the day. Saturdays between June and September go first.

  2. 02

    When do we get the photographs?

    Thirty preview frames within 72 hours, the full gallery in four weeks, the album about six weeks after you choose its pictures.

  3. 03

    What if it rains?

    From November to March it usually does, around three. We plan portraits for the morning and keep a covered spot in reserve. Rain photographs well.

  4. 04

    Do you shoot film?

    One or two rolls of 35 mm at every wedding, included. They're scanned and delivered with the rest of the gallery.

  5. 05

    Do you travel?

    Yes. Outside Bandung Raya I add transport and one night's stay at cost, with nothing on top.

  6. 06

    How does payment work?

    A 30% deposit by bank transfer holds the date. The balance is due two weeks before the wedding or on the day of a portrait session.
