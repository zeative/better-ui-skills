Questions

Before you send invoice number one.

Rather ask a person?Joss from support replies within a working day. hello@ledgerly.example
01Do my clients need a Ledgerly account to pay?

No. They get an email with a link, open the invoice in their browser and pay by card or bank transfer. Nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

02What does it cost when I get paid?

Card payments are 2.9% + 30¢. Bank transfers are 0.8%, capped at $5. The fees are the same on every plan, and you can pass the card fee on to the client if you prefer.

03Will reminders sound like a robot wrote them?

They are your words. Edit the three templates once, pick the days they go out, and Ledgerly sends them from your own email address. Any client can be paused with one click.

04Can I bring my old invoices with me?

Yes. Import clients and past invoices from a CSV or spreadsheet, and numbering carries on from your last invoice.

05What happens if I cancel?

You drop to Starter and keep every invoice you've sent. You can export everything as PDF and CSV at any time.
