04A · Who's behind the camera

I grew up in Cihapit, learned on my father's film camera, and still shoot as if every roll has only 36 frames.

That habit is why I don't line your family up for an hour, and why you won't hear me say “one more”. I watch, I wait where the light is good, and I press the shutter when something true happens: your father straightening his beskap, your grandmother laughing at the saweran coins.

I know the adat — Sundanese, Javanese, Minang — well enough to be standing in the right place before the moment starts. Bahasa Indonesia, Basa Sunda and English are all fine.

01How many photographs do we get, and when?

Twenty previews within 48 hours, so you have something to post while it's still fresh. The full gallery follows within 21 days: 250+ frames for an akad, 600+ for a full day. Every frame is edited by me, one at a time.

02What if it rains? This is Bandung.

From November to March it usually does, around three in the afternoon. I plan outdoor portraits for the morning, scout a covered spot at every venue, and carry clear umbrellas. Some of my favourite frames are the wet ones. Outdoor pre-wedding sessions get one free reschedule.

03Do you travel outside Bandung?

Yes. Bandung Raya, Lembang, Ciwidey and Pangalengan are included. For Jakarta, Yogyakarta, Bali and anywhere else, transport and one night's stay are added at cost, with receipts.

04Will you direct us, or just follow?

Mostly follow. For the family formals I work from a list you send me, and it takes about twenty minutes. For portraits of the two of you I'll give you something to do, not a pose to hold.

05How do we book a date?

Send the date and venue. If I'm free I'll hold it for seven days at no cost while we talk. A signed agreement and a 30% DP confirm it; the balance is due seven days before the wedding.

06Can we have the RAW files?

No. A RAW file is half a photograph. You get every finished frame in full resolution, with a licence to print and share them wherever you like.
