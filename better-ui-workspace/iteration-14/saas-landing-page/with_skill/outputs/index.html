FAQ

Questions before you start

Short answers to what freelancers ask us most.

Still have a question?

Our support team replies within a working day. Email support

Do my clients need a Ledgerly account to pay?

No. Clients get an email with a link to the invoice and pay by card or bank transfer without signing up. They can also download a PDF for their records.

How quickly does money reach my bank account?

Card and bank payments are paid out to your account two business days after the client pays. You can see every payout and its status under Reports.

Can I import clients and invoices from another tool?

Yes. Upload a CSV of clients and past invoices and Ledgerly matches the columns for you. Paid invoices are kept for your income reports.

Does Ledgerly file my taxes?

No. It estimates what to set aside and prepares a summary and transaction export that you or your accountant can use to file.

What happens if I downgrade to Starter?

Nothing is deleted. You keep all past invoices and reports, and can keep billing your three most recent clients. Reminders and time tracking pause until you upgrade again.
