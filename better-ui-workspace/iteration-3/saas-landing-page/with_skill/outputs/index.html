02 / Cash flow

See next month’s rent before it lands.

Irregular income is easier to live with when you can see it coming. Ledgerly lines up what has been paid, what is owed and when each invoice falls due.

  • A due date for every dollar

    The list of what comes in next, ordered by date, with late ones flagged.

  • A tidy export at year end

    Send your accountant one CSV or PDF pack with every invoice, payment and expense.

05 / Questions

Before you send the first one.

The things freelancers usually ask before moving their invoicing.

Something else?Write to hello@ledgerly.example
01Is the Starter plan really free?

Yes. You can invoice up to three active clients for as long as you like, with payments and read receipts included. You only pay when you need more clients or want reminders, time tracking and the tax view.

02Do my clients need a Ledgerly account to pay?

No. They open the invoice from the email or link and pay from that page by card or bank transfer. Nothing to sign up for.

03Can I bring my existing clients and invoices?

You can import clients and past invoices from a CSV file, so your numbering carries on from where you left off.

04How do reminders work, and can I turn them off for a client?

You choose when reminders go out, such as on the due date and a week after, and you can edit the wording. They can be paused for a single invoice or switched off for a client.

05Does Ledgerly file my taxes?

No. Ledgerly shows how much to set aside based on the rate you enter and gives your accountant a clean export. It is not tax advice and it does not file anything for you.

06What happens if I cancel?

Your account moves to Starter. Your invoices stay available and you can export everything at any time.
