Questions

Asked before signing up.

Something else? Write to usA person replies within one working day.
Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Starter has no time limit and no card on file. You pay only the payment processor’s fee when a client pays by card.

How do my clients pay?

Each invoice email carries a pay link. Clients choose card, bank transfer or a wallet and never need a Ledgerly account. Funds reach your bank in about two business days.

Will the reminders sound like a robot?

They are sent from your name, with wording you can edit once and reuse. You choose how many, how far apart, and which clients never get them.

Does Ledgerly file my taxes?

No. It estimates what to set aside from each payment at a rate you choose and gives your accountant a clean export. It is not tax advice.

Can I bring invoices from my current tool?

Import clients and past invoices from a CSV. Your numbering carries on from wherever you left off.
