How it’s made

Stitched, not just glued, so it can be resoled

Most court sneakers are cemented together and thrown away when the tread goes. The Halden’s cupsole is stitched to the upper all the way around, so a cobbler can replace it when you have worn through the first one.

The leather comes from a tannery in León, Mexico, audited gold by the Leather Working Group. It is 1.6 mm thick, left uncoated, and it creases and darkens with wear instead of cracking.

Inside there is a calfskin lining and a 4 mm cork footbed. Give it about a week; after that it fits your foot and nobody else’s.

Care

Wipe the leather with a damp cloth and let it dry away from heat. Brush the nubuck dry with a soft brush; do not use water on it.

Condition the leather every few months. Do not machine wash.
