About

I'd rather you forget I'm there than pose for me.

Halo, I'm Raka. I grew up in Bandung, and the light here — cool mornings, mist coming off the hills, long soft afternoons — shaped the way I see. I work mostly with available light and one or two small cameras, so I can stay close without getting in the way.

At weddings I watch for the things between the big moments: your mother fixing your kebaya, friends crying at the back, the look just after the akad. For portraits, we talk first and photograph second. Nobody is good at being photographed. That's my job, not yours.

— Raka
