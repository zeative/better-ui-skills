About Raka

I grew up in Bandung and started out photographing friends' bands in Dago. A cousin asked me to cover her wedding in 2016, and I have been doing this full time ever since.

I work alone or with one second shooter, carry two cameras and very little else, and stay from the morning preparations until the last guests leave. You get the whole day back, not only the posed parts.
