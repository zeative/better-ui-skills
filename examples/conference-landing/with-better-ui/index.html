03 Venue

A 1950s print hall in Cikini

600 seats in the main hall, two studios for workshops and a roof terrace for the evenings.

Gedung Tjikini 17

Jl. Cikini Raya No. 17, Menteng
Jakarta Pusat 10330

  • Train

    KRL Commuter Line to Cikini station, then 6 minutes on foot

  • MRT

    Bundaran HI station, then 10 minutes by taxi or ojek

  • Airport

    Soekarno-Hatta, about 60 minutes by car outside rush hour
