Titik temu means meeting point. This is the one where craft meets the city it has to work in.

Slow networks, bright sun, crowded platforms, four scripts on one sign. We invited designers who treat those conditions as the brief rather than the excuse, and asked them to show their working.

Venue

Cikini, Central Jakarta

Jakarta's arts centre since 1968: theatres, a planetarium, a public library and the art school next door.

Taman Ismail Marzuki

Jl. Cikini Raya No. 73
Menteng, Jakarta Pusat 10330
Indonesia
Open in Maps ↗

Talks run in the main theatre; workshops and lunch spill into the courtyard and the library building. Everything is within a two-minute walk.

By train
KRL Commuter Line to Cikini station, then about 15 minutes on foot along Jl. Cikini Raya.
By bus
Transjakarta services stop on Jl. Cikini Raya, outside the main gate.
By car or ojek
Drop-off at the main gate. On-site parking is limited, so ride-hailing is easier.
From the airport
Soekarno–Hatta (CGK) is 45 to 90 minutes away by car, depending on traffic.
Access
Step-free entry and seating, accessible toilets, a quiet room and a prayer room on site.

FAQ

Before you ask

What language are the talks in?

Some are in English, some in Bahasa Indonesia. Every talk has live captions in both languages on screens beside the stage.

Is the food halal? Are there vegetarian options?

All catering is halal. Vegetarian and vegan plates are available at every meal, and you can tell us about allergies when you book.

Can my company pay by invoice?

Yes. Choose the team ticket or email us with your company details and NPWP, and we'll send an invoice with a faktur pajak.

Will the talks be recorded?

Talks are recorded and sent to General and Team ticket holders in May. Workshops and portfolio reviews are not recorded.

I can't make it any more. What happens to my ticket?

You get a full refund until 16 March 2027. After that you can transfer the ticket to another person for free, up to the day before.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, and it applies to everyone: attendees, speakers, staff and sponsors. Our volunteers wear marigold lanyards and can be reached at any time during the event.
