Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Write to us and a human will reply.

Who counts as an editor?

Anyone who can create or change designs in your workspace. People who only view files, leave comments, play prototypes or inspect specs are free on every plan, and there's no limit on how many you invite.

How does yearly billing work?

You pay for twelve months up front at a 20% discount: $144 per editor on Pro, $336 per editor on Studio. If you add an editor partway through the year, we charge a prorated amount for the months remaining so everyone renews on the same date.

Can I switch plans or billing period later?

Yes, at any time from workspace settings. Upgrades take effect immediately and you pay only the prorated difference. Downgrades and switches from yearly to monthly take effect at the end of your current billing period.

What happens to my files if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted. Your workspace moves to the Free plan, and files beyond the three-file limit become view-only. You can still open, export and share them, and they unlock again if you resubscribe.

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

No. Pro and Studio trials run for 14 days with every feature unlocked and no card on file. If you don't pick a plan when the trial ends, the workspace drops back to Free.

Do you offer discounts for students or nonprofits?

Pro is free for students and educators with a verified school email. Registered nonprofits get 50% off Pro and Studio on either billing period.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards. Studio workspaces on yearly billing with ten or more editors can also pay by invoice and bank transfer.
