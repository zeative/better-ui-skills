Questions before you pay

Seats, trials, discounts and cancelling, answered with the actual numbers.

Who counts as an editor?

Anyone who can change a file: move a layer, edit a component, publish a library. That is the only paid seat.

People who view, comment, play prototypes or use code inspect are free on every plan, with no limit on how many you invite.

What happens when I add or remove an editor?

A new editor is charged from the day they join until the end of the billing period. On Pro monthly that is $0.50 a day.

When you remove an editor, the unused days come back as credit on your next invoice.

Can I try Pro before paying?

Yes. The trial runs for 14 days with every Pro feature and does not ask for a card.

If you do not subscribe, the workspace moves to Free. Nothing is deleted: files beyond the first three become read-only until you upgrade or export them.

How does yearly billing work?

You pay for 12 months up front at a lower rate: $12 instead of $15 per editor on Pro, and $36 instead of $45 on Organization. That is $36 and $108 saved per editor each year.

Editors added during the year are charged for the months remaining, at the yearly rate.

Are there discounts for students and non-profits?

Students and teachers get Pro for free after verifying a school email address, renewed each academic year.

Registered non-profits pay half price on Pro and Organization. Apply for a discount

How do I cancel?

In Settings, under Billing, at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, then the workspace moves to Free.

Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of payment.

How can I pay?

Visa, Mastercard and American Express on Pro and Organization.

Organization workspaces on yearly billing can also pay by invoice and bank transfer, due in 30 days.
