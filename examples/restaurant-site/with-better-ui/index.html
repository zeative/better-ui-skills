02板前

Raka Wiratama, behind the counter

Raka grew up above his mother's rice stall in Cihapit market. At twenty-three he left for Kanazawa, where he spent his first year at a kappo restaurant washing rice and nothing else. He stayed nine years and left as second to the head chef.

He came home in 2019 and opened Kiri two years later, in a 1930s house on Jalan Progo. The technique is the one he was taught. The fish comes up from Benoa overnight, and nearly everything else grows within an hour of the door.

“I cook what I learned in Japan with what the hills here give me.”
