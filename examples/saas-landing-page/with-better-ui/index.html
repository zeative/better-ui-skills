FAQ

Questions before you start

Can't find an answer? Email help@ledgerly.app and a person replies within a working day.

Do my clients need a Ledgerly account to pay?

No. They get an email with a link to the invoice, and can pay by card or bank transfer from any device without signing up.

How long until the money reaches my bank?

Card payments arrive in 2 business days. Bank transfers take 3 to 5 business days, depending on your bank.

Can I bring my existing clients and invoices?

Yes. Import clients from a CSV file, and upload past invoices as PDFs so your income history stays in one place.

What happens if I cancel?

Your account moves to the Starter plan. You keep every invoice and can export all your data at any time.
