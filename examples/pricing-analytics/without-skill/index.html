How we count a tracked user

One person, counted once per calendar month, however much they do.

01

One person, one count

Someone who sends one event and someone who sends ten thousand each count as a single tracked user. Events are never metered.

02

Sign-ins don't double up

When an anonymous visitor logs in, the two profiles merge into one. The same person on phone and laptop is still one user once identified.

03

Spikes are forgiven

Go over your tier and we keep collecting everything. Your plan only moves up after two months in a row above the limit.

Questions about billing

Something else? Email billing@sightline.example and a person will reply.

What exactly is a monthly tracked user?

A monthly tracked user is one unique person who sends at least one event to Sightline during a calendar month. They are counted once, no matter how many events, sessions or devices are involved.

The count resets on the first of each month. Someone who was active in March but not in April doesn't count in April.

Do anonymous visitors count?

Yes. Anyone who triggers an event counts, whether or not they have signed in. When an anonymous visitor later identifies themselves, we merge the two profiles and count them once for that month.

If you only care about signed-in usage, you can turn off anonymous tracking per project and those visitors will not be counted.

What happens if I go over my plan's limit?

Nothing stops. We keep collecting and showing all of your data, and there are no overage fees for a single busy month.

If you are over the limit two months in a row, we email you and move you to the tier that matches your usage from the next billing cycle. On the Free plan, we keep collecting for 30 days and ask you to upgrade to keep seeing new data.

Are bots and my own team counted?

No. Known bots and crawlers are filtered before counting. You can also exclude your team by email domain or IP range, and test projects are free and never count towards your total.

Is there really no limit on events?

Events are not metered or billed. The only guard rail is a fair-use ceiling of 1,000 events per tracked user per month, averaged across your project. Most products send between 50 and 200. If you get close, we will contact you before anything changes.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are paid up front for twelve months at 20% less than the monthly rate. You commit to a tracked-user tier for the year. If you grow past it, you pay the difference for the remaining months only.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time from billing settings. Upgrades apply straight away and are prorated. Downgrades and cancellations apply at the end of the current billing period, and your data stays available on the Free plan within its 90-day history.

Do you offer discounts for startups or non-profits?

Companies under two years old with less than $5 million raised get the Growth plan free for a year, up to 50,000 tracked users. Registered non-profits and open-source projects get 50% off any paid plan. Apply here.
